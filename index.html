Common questions

Why are there no prices on the website?

Medicine prices change with new stock and company revisions. Instead of showing an old price, we confirm the current price of every item in your WhatsApp order before you pay anything.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery, or cash at the counter if you pick up. There is no online payment and nothing is charged from this website.

Do you deliver outside Gulistan Society?

Yes, across Dera Ismail Khan. Write your full address in the cart — delivery time and charges for your area are confirmed in the WhatsApp reply.

Can I order something that is not listed here?

Yes. The shop shows our everyday range, but the store stocks much more. Message the product name or a photo of the pack on WhatsApp and we will check the shelf.

Which items need a prescription?

Items marked "Prescription" in red. Add them to your order as normal and share a photo of your valid prescription in the same WhatsApp chat.
