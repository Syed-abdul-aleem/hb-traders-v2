About HB Traders

A neighbourhood pharmacy inside Gulistan Society, Dera Ismail Khan — genuine medicines, straight answers, and now ordering on WhatsApp.

Why ordering moved to WhatsApp

Most of our customers already had our number saved. They would message a medicine name, wait for a reply, then ask about the pack size, then the price — five or six messages for one order. This website fixes that: the shelf is in front of you with full details of every product, your cart turns into one complete WhatsApp message, and we confirm the current prices in the reply before anything is packed.

What we stock

Everyday pain relief and cold-and-flu medicines, vitamins and supplements, acidity and digestive care, first-aid essentials, baby food and diapers, personal care, and home health devices like sugar-testing kits and thermometers. Prescription medicines are dispensed against a valid prescription — send a photo of it on WhatsApp and we prepare your order.

How we check every order

Every order is checked at the counter against your WhatsApp message — right product, right strength, right quantity — before it leaves. If something is out of stock, we tell you in the chat before you pay, and only suggest an alternative if you ask for one.

Why there are no prices on this site

Medicine prices change whenever new stock arrives at revised company rates. Rather than display a price that may be out of date, we confirm the exact current price of each item in your WhatsApp order. You see the full bill first, and pay only when you are satisfied — cash on delivery or at pickup.
